# TANKA - Brand Guidelines

## Core Identity
**Brand Name:** Tanka
**Website:** tanka.money
**Tagline:** Mine it. Send it. Spend it.

## Colours
- **Midnight Navy:** `#0B1933` (RGB: 11, 25, 51)
- **Tanka Cyan:** `#12DCE6` (RGB: 18, 220, 230)
- **Silver:** `#BAC8D6` (RGB: 186, 200, 214)

## Typography
- Wordmark uses a clean, geometric sans-serif typeface in bold, all lowercase.
- Tagline uses regular weight, sentence case.

## Logos and Assets
- Use SVG files for scalable print and high-DPI digital formats.
- Use PNG files with transparent backgrounds for websites and marketing.
- Use specific exchange assets for 32px to 512px icon listings.
- Maintain consistent spacing; do not warp or apply arbitrary shadow effects.

## Usage
- **Dark Mode:** Prefer the Midnight Navy background to make the Cyan pop.
- **Light Mode:** Use the Navy wordmark on a white background.
- **Micro Scaling:** The 16px and 24px icon sizes remain legible due to the clean geometric paths.
